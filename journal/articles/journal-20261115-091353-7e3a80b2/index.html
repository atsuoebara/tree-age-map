Rings People’s Steve Speirs Runs Sub-3 at 60—and Wins His Age Group in Richmond

Rings People guest Steve Speirs ran 2:57:13 at the 2026 Richmond Marathon, breaking three hours at age 60 and winning his age group. A remarkable story 27 years after his first U.S. marathon in Richmond.

Wonderful news from a member of our Rings People community. Steve Speirs, our second Rings People guest, broke three hours at age 60 at the Richmond Marathon in Richmond, Virginia, USA, on November 14, 2026. His chip time was 2:57:13 (gun time 2:57:21). Wearing bib 1784, Steve finished 197th overall out of 5,466 runners and first among 87 men aged 60–64. ■ A negative split on the way to 2:57:13 Steve reached 10K in 42:24, halfway in 1:29:16, and 20 miles in 2:15:39 before finishing in 2:57:13. His second half was 1:27:57—faster than the first. A negative split to complete an extraordinary race. ■ Richmond, 27 years later In November 1999, Steve ran his first marathon in the United States in Richmond. His time then was 3:05:52. Twenty-seven years later, at 60, he returned to the same race and finished in 2:57:13—8 minutes and 39 seconds faster. Two Richmond finishes, 27 years apart. The story is about more than a finishing time: it is about all the running between them. ■ Twenty-one consecutive years of sub-three marathons—and a new milestone In his Rings People interview, Steve shared that he has been running since he was 16. From 2005 through 2025, he completed a sub-three-hour marathon every year for 21 consecutive years. Now, at age 60, he has done it again in Richmond. After the race, Steve wrote: “Thankful for all the support this morning.” Responding to congratulations on November 15, he added: “Tough race, but really pleased with the result. Thanks!!” His words make clear that this was no easy day—and how much the result means. Congratulations, Steve, on your sub-three marathon at 60 and your age-group victory! Every run, every year, every challenge adds another ring. That is the spirit of Runner’s Rings.
